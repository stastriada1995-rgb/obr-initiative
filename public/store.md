# Initiative & Actions Tracker

_A combat tracker for D&D 5e that keeps the whole action economy in one place._

> The interface is in **Russian**.

![Tracker](https://stastriada1995-rgb.github.io/obr-initiative/screenshots/tracker-gm.png)

## Features

- **Initiative order** with a d20 roll button that adds the token's initiative bonus.
- **Action / Bonus action / Reaction** markers for every combatant, restored at the start of each round. Actions and bonus actions can only be spent on the creature's own turn.
- **Skills and spells** stored on the token, color-coded by type: action, bonus action, reaction, free action, passive. Spells get their own shade.
- **Resources** such as ki, spell slots or arrows. Using a skill spends its action, resource and charge in one click.
- **Recharge**: every round, every other round, short rest, long rest, manual, or a dice roll ("Recharge 5–6") made automatically at the start of the creature's turn.
- **D&D 5e conditions** with optional duration in rounds and exhaustion levels.
- **Resistances, immunities and vulnerabilities** listed first in the character's drawer.
- **HP, temporary HP and AC**: damage, healing and temp HP from the tracker, and an HP bar with an AC badge above the token on the map.
- **Undo**: step back one turn and roll back everything clicked since.
- **Player view**: players only see the turn order, names, conditions and the round number. The GM sees everything.

## How to use

1. Right-click a character token and choose **Добавить в инициативу** (Add to initiative).
2. Right-click a token and choose **Навыки персонажа** (Character skills) to set AC, HP, initiative bonus, defenses, resources and skills.
3. Open the tracker from the extension icon, roll initiative and press **Следующий ход** (Next turn).
